---
title: Reviewing AI changes
description: See every edit an AI agent made, in the Grid, the Line editor and the Review panel, and keep it, undo it, or go back to before a turn.
---

An edit an AI agent makes goes into the file at once, but HikariSub marks it until you look at it. Each one waits until you **Keep** it or **Undo** it. These edits come from HikariSub's own [Agent panel](/en/docs/ai/agent), whose turns are named *AI*, and from an agent you [connect over MCP](/en/docs/ai/connect-an-agent), such as Claude Code. Plugins' edits aren't reviewed: they go into the history as ordinary steps.

## Seeing what changed

- **In the Grid,** a line with a waiting change has a stripe at the left of its **#** column, in the accent colour. It turns to the warning colour once you edit the line yourself. The words the AI added are tinted and underlined in green.
- **In the Line editor,** a strip below the tools says *Changed by* and the agent's name, such as *Changed by Claude Code*, with the change's name, **Undo** and **Keep**. The arrows and *2 of 5* go through the waiting lines, and wrap at the ends. Below, the text shows added words in green and removed ones struck through. For a line the agent added, it says *The AI added this line.*
- **On the tab,** a badge counts the changes waiting there.
- **In the status bar,** *3 AI changes to review* counts them for the current tab. Click it to open the Review panel.

## The Review panel

Open it with **Edit › Review › Show panel**, from **View › Panels › Review**, from the count in the status bar, or with **Show in Review panel** in the Grid's right-click menu.

It lists the current tab's changes, newest turn first. Each **turn** shows its number, such as *Turn 3*, the agent's name and **Restore checkpoint**. Under it, each change the agent made has **Undo** and **Keep**, and under that each line, style or script property it touched, with its own **Undo** and **Keep**. A line you edited after the agent says *you edited it since*. Click a line to select it in the Grid.

**Keep all** and **Undo all**, at the top, act on every waiting change in the tab. **Also to review**, at the bottom, lists the other tabs with waiting changes, each with **Go to tab**.

With nothing waiting, the panel says *Changes the AI makes to this tab show here until you keep or undo them.*

## Keep and Undo

**Keep** accepts the change. The file doesn't change, the marks go away, and no history step is added.

**Undo** puts back exactly what was there before the agent, as one history step, such as *Undo Claude Code change to Line 12*. A line the agent added is removed, and a line it deleted or moved comes back where it was. Styles and script properties go back too.

If you edited the line after the agent did, Undo would drop your edit too, so it asks first: **Undo anyway** or **Cancel**. **Undo all** leaves those lines as they are, unless you choose **Undo all anyway**; **Leave my edits** undoes the rest.

Ordinary **Edit › Undo** works too: undoing the agent's own step in the history takes it out of the review, and Redo brings it back.

| Command | Key at first | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Keep AI change** | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> | Keeps the waiting changes on the current line |
| **Undo AI change** | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Backspace</kbd> | Undoes them |
| **Next AI change** | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Down</kbd> | Goes to the next waiting line |
| **Previous AI change** | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Up</kbd> | Goes to the one before |
| **Keep all AI changes** | | Keeps every waiting change in the tab |
| **Undo all AI changes** | | Undoes them all |
| **Review panel** | | Shows the panel |

They are under **Edit › Review**, and change their keys in **File › Settings › Shortcuts**. On a waiting line, the Grid's right-click menu starts with **Keep AI change**, **Undo AI change**, **Next AI change** and **Show in Review panel**.

## Checkpoints

Each turn starts with a checkpoint: the files as they were before its first change. The **History** shows *Before turn 3* above that turn's first step, and marks the agent's steps with an AI icon.

**Restore checkpoint**, on a turn in the Review panel, goes back to before it. The question says what it will undo in each tab: *Every AI change from this turn on is undone, kept ones too, in every tab it touched, as one history step per tab.* Lines you edited after the agent stay as they are, unless you tick **Also undo my edits to 2 lines I changed after the AI**. Your other edits stay. Press **Restore**; Undo brings the agent's changes back.

## Saving, closing and turning AI off

- Saving writes the file with the agent's edits in it. They keep waiting in the review.
- The review isn't saved with the file. Closing a file drops its review without asking, and the agent's edits stay in it as ordinary text.
- **Turn off all AI features** hides the marks, the Review panel, **Edit › Review** and its commands. It doesn't keep or undo anything: when AI is back on, the changes wait again.
