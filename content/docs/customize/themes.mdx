Themes change only HikariSub's window. Your subtitles, their styles and the video look the same in every theme.

## Theme

**Interface theme** offers four: **Light**, **Dark**, **High contrast white** and **High contrast black**. Each card is a small sample of it.

**Follow system theme**, on at first, uses Light or Dark as your system's colour scheme does, and changes when it does. With a high-contrast theme chosen, it switches between High contrast white and High contrast black instead. Clicking a card turns it off and keeps that theme.

A [plugin](/en/docs/plugins/using-plugins) can bring colour themes. While it's turned on, its themes follow the built-in cards. When it's turned off or uninstalled, a window showing one of its themes switches to the built-in theme of the same kind, such as Dark for a dark theme. To make a theme of your own, start from the one you see: **Save a copy as a theme plugin…** in the plugin manager saves it as a plugin you can edit. See [Data-only packages](/en/docs/plugins/write-your-first-plugin#data-only-packages).

On Linux desktops that don't tell Qt their scheme, such as sway or Hyprland, HikariSub asks the desktop portal. If the scheme can't be found at all, the chosen theme shows, Dark at first.

## Accent colour

The accent marks the selection, default buttons, switches that are on and the field you are typing in. Light and Dark offer seven accents each, all readable on their theme: red, orange, gold, green (the default), blue, purple and pink. Hover one to see its contrast.

Light and Dark keep their own accent. To change the other theme's, show that theme first.

## High contrast colours

With a high-contrast theme shown, three colours replace the accent: **Accent colour**, **Text and icons**, and **Border colour**, which outlines every control. **Choose…** picks one, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> in the picker puts back the colour from before. Text on the accent turns black or white by itself to stay readable. **Reset to default** puts back that theme's three colours.

## Icons

A [plugin](/en/docs/plugins/using-plugins) can bring an icon theme. Once a plugin you've turned on has one, **Appearance** gets an **Icons** section, after the accent colours. Its **Icons** row offers **HikariSub**, the default, and each plugin icon theme, named after the theme and its plugin, such as *Round — Looks*. A change applies at once.

An icon theme needn't cover every icon: the ones it leaves out show HikariSub's own. When the plugin is turned off or uninstalled, HikariSub's icons come back.

## Font

**Program font** and its size set the font of the whole interface, 10-point Tahoma at first. A change applies to every window at once, without restarting. It doesn't change your subtitles' fonts. Type in the box to search your fonts, as in the [Style manager's Family](/en/docs/basics/styles#editing-a-style).

## Title bar

HikariSub draws its own title bar, with the menus and the layout button in it, on Windows, KDE Plasma and GNOME. Elsewhere, and on tiling window managers such as sway, i3 or Hyprland, it uses your system's title bar, with the menus in the first row of the window.

**Use system title bar** changes this for the main window and every dialog. On Windows, the system's title bars follow the theme's light or dark side; on Linux they follow your desktop's theme.

## Resetting

**Reset page…**, in the right-click menu of **Appearance** in the Settings window's contents list, puts back every appearance setting and the font. **Reset all…** leaves the appearance alone.

Themes from older versions of HikariSub are not imported.
