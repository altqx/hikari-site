---
title: Fix minor errors (removed)
description: Fix minor errors is removed after 0.1.0-beta.3. What happens to its panel, rules and shortcut, and what to use instead.
---

Fix minor errors is removed from HikariSub after 0.1.0-beta.3: the panel, **Edit › Fix minor errors (experimental)** and **View › Panels › Fix minor errors** are gone.

- A saved layout, session or subtitle file that had the panel opens without it.
- A shortcut you gave it is dropped, and the Log says so.
- Your rules stay in `rules.toml` in your [user folder](/en/docs/automation/user-folder), untouched and unused. While the file is there, the Log mentions it at each start: *Fix minor errors was removed. Your rules stay in …, unused.* Delete it when you no longer need it.
- [Import legacy settings](/en/docs/get-started/moving-from-legacy) no longer brings over legacy's `Rules.txt`. It lists those rules, and a shortcut legacy had for the command, as Retired.

To fix the same kind of mistake, use [Find and replace](/en/docs/basics/find-and-replace) with a [regular expression](/en/docs/basics/find-and-replace#regular-expressions) and **Replace all**.
