### Introducing

#### One Settings window

- **Settings is one window, and every change applies at once:** there's no OK, Cancel or Apply. Search finds a setting by its name, its note or its id, and **Filter** narrows the list to the settings you changed or a plugin's. Every row has a dot when it's changed, a reset button and a **⋯** menu with **Copy link**, and **Reset page…** and **Reset all…** ask first. See [Settings](/en/docs/customize/settings).
- **Yours and the Project's:** the **Yours** tab holds your settings, and in a Project the **Project** tab holds what the Project sets, with the same pages. A row the Project sets says so on Yours. The "Mine" tier is now called "Yours" everywhere. See [Yours and Project](/en/docs/customize/settings#yours-and-project).
- **Your settings file:** **Open settings file** opens `settings.toml`, and what you change there shows in the window at once. `hikarisub://settings/…` links open the window at a setting. See [Setting links](/en/docs/customize/settings#setting-links).
- **Plugin settings** are under **File › Settings › Plugins**, one page for each plugin. See [Plugin settings](/en/docs/customize/settings#plugin-settings).

#### Typesetting in full screen

- **Simple full screen:** the picture sits above a black strip, so nothing covers the frame. The strip has the time, a seek bar with the keyframes marked, the current line, and the frame, play and stop buttons. It hides 2 seconds after you stop moving the pointer while the video plays, and its right-click menu and **File › Settings › Video › Full screen** choose where it sits and when it shows. A frame that can't be decoded says so on the picture. See [Full screen](/en/docs/basics/video#full-screen).
- **Full-screen typesetting:** **Typeset** on the strip, or **View › Full screen typesetting**, puts the visual tools beside the picture: the tools by name on the left, the tool's fields on the right, and the active line under the picture, where you can edit it. Edit shapes and Edit tags open beside the picture, and each tool's **?** opens its guide. See [Full-screen typesetting](/en/docs/basics/video#full-screen-typesetting).
- **Step through lines:** in the Styles panel and in full-screen typesetting, <kbd>Enter</kbd> gives the active line a style and goes to the next line, as Aegisub's Styling Assistant does. See [Choosing a style](/en/docs/basics/video#choosing-a-style).

#### The command palette

- **The command palette and Quick Open:** run any command by its name, open a recent or Project file, or jump to a line, a time, a style or a panel, from one box at the top of the window. Macros that can't run on the selection are greyed out with the reason, as in the Automation menu. See [Command palette and Quick Open](/en/docs/basics/command-palette).
- **The same box elsewhere:** choosing an MKV's audio or subtitle track, and renaming an agent thread or a node, use the same box: type to filter, <kbd>Esc</kbd> to cancel. See [The same box elsewhere](/en/docs/basics/command-palette#the-same-box-elsewhere).

#### Before export

- **Steps before export:** **File › Export as…** can run steps on a copy before it writes, as Aegisub's export filters do: apply karaoke templates, transform the frame rate, remove tags, set unknown styles to Default, or run a script's own filters. Save a list of steps as a recipe, for you or the Project, or use the list Aegisub keeps in the file. A step that fails stops the export, and nothing is written. See [Before export](/en/docs/basics/export#before-export).

#### Files in other encodings

- **Files that aren't UTF-8:** a bar names the encoding HikariSub found, and **File › Reopen with encoding…** reads the file in any of 17 encodings. Saving writes UTF-8. See [Files that aren't UTF-8](/en/docs/basics/files-and-formats#files-that-arent-utf-8).
- **Export in another encoding:** SRT, YouTube SBV, plain text, MicroDVD, MPL2 and TMPlayer can be written in another encoding, and HikariSub asks first when some characters don't fit. See [Encoding](/en/docs/basics/export#encoding).
- **Import text:** **File › Import text…** turns a plain-text script, one line each, such as `Ana: Hello`, into untimed lines, after the last line of the open file or in a new file, with a preview as you change the options. See [Importing text](/en/docs/basics/files-and-formats#importing-text).

#### Plugins

- **Menus, the status bar and shortcuts:** plugins can add rows to HikariSub's menus, items to the status bar and default keyboard shortcuts. Right-click the status bar to hide an item. A default you clear stays cleared. See [Running a plugin's commands](/en/docs/plugins/using-plugins#running-a-plugins-commands).
- **Lists and questions:** a plugin can ask you to pick from a list or type a value, in the same box as the command palette, but only in answer to something you did. See [Lists and questions](/en/docs/plugins/using-plugins#lists-and-questions).
- **Styles, presets and more from plugins:** plugins can bring style catalogues, karaoke presets, node-effect graphs, spelling dictionaries and layouts, with no code. They're read-only, you can copy them to change them, and they go when the plugin is turned off. See [Styles, presets and more from plugins](/en/docs/plugins/using-plugins#styles-presets-and-more-from-plugins).
- **Subtitle formats from plugins:** a plugin's formats show in **Export as** and **Save as**, and its readers open files the built-in ones can't. See [Subtitle formats from plugins](/en/docs/plugins/using-plugins#subtitle-formats-from-plugins).
- **Programs:** a plugin can be a program in any language, in its own sandbox, and can have HikariSub download programs for it, checked against the size and SHA-256 the plugin names. Nothing is downloaded while **Let plugins connect to the internet** is off. See [Programs](/en/docs/plugins/using-plugins#programs).
- **MCP servers from plugins** show in **File › Settings › AI**, marked with the plugin, and are off until you turn them on. See [MCP servers](/en/docs/plugins/using-plugins#mcp-servers).
- **Themes and icons from plugins:** a plugin's colour themes join **File › Settings › Appearance**, and an icon theme adds an **Icons** section there. A plugin's own icons can show on its commands and status bar items. See [Themes and icons from plugins](/en/docs/plugins/using-plugins#themes-and-icons-from-plugins).
- **For plugin authors:** load a plugin straight from its folder with **Install dev plugin…**. It reloads whenever its files change, and keeps its own data. See [Work on it in place](/en/docs/plugins/write-your-first-plugin#work-on-it-in-place). Plugins can also suggest text, explain it and report problems in the Line editor, and there are type hints for Lua and TypeScript, with a TypeScript sample.

### New and improved

#### AI

- **AI is off when you first install HikariSub,** and nothing for it downloads or starts until you turn it on. Turning it on installs the AI component at once. If you had a provider or the AI component, AI stays on. See [Turning AI on](/en/docs/ai/settings#turning-ai-on).
- **The model picker:** the Agent panel's model button opens a list you can search, grouped by agent and provider, with your recent models first, and **File › Settings › AI** uses the same list. A model you can't use yet says why. A provider's models show as soon as you add it or sign in. See [Choosing a model](/en/docs/ai/agent#choosing-a-model).
- **The AI component's state** shows in **File › Settings › AI**, with **Install** and **Retry**. See [AI components](/en/docs/ai/settings#ai-components).
- **Suggest synonyms:** **AI actions › Suggest synonyms** in the Line editor offers up to 8 words for the word at the cursor, and picking one replaces only that word. See [Suggesting synonyms](/en/docs/ai/inline-actions#suggesting-synonyms).

#### Grid and Line editor

- **Reading limits:** set a CPS warning and a CPS error level, the characters per line and the number of lines in **File › Settings › Grid › Counting**. The Grid's CPS cell shades from warning to error, as in Aegisub, and a file can keep its own limits in **Subtitles › Script properties**. See [Reading limits](/en/docs/basics/grid#reading-limits).
- **Problems:** the QC notes panel is now **Problems**, and lists the lines past your reading limits and what plugins find, beside your QC notes. Filter by how serious they are or where they come from, and run a quick fix from the row's menu or with **Quick fix** (<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd>). In the Line editor, a wavy line marks where a plugin's problem is. See [Problems](/en/docs/checking/qc-notes#problems) and [the Line editor's problems](/en/docs/basics/line-editor#problems).
- **Suggestions as you type:** plugins' suggestions join the tag list in one list, which opens on `\`, a plugin's own character, or **Trigger completion** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Space</kbd>). Pointing at text shows what a plugin says about it. See [The text](/en/docs/basics/line-editor#the-text).
- **Filter the Grid as you type:** the Grid's header has a filter field. It shows only the lines whose text, actor, effect or style has what you typed, and changes nothing in the file. See [Hiding lines](/en/docs/basics/grid#hiding-lines).

#### Files and fonts

- **Resample letterboxed and cropped video:** **Subtitles › Resample subtitles…** has **Letterbox** and **Crop**, or edges you type, so signs stay on what they mark. It can also change the YCbCr matrix, converting the colours so they look the same. See [Resolution](/en/docs/basics/files-and-formats#resolution).
- **A different matrix on open:** when a video's YCbCr matrix differs from the subtitles', the question also offers to convert the colours, as one undo step. See [Resolution](/en/docs/basics/files-and-formats#resolution).
- **Link fonts to a folder:** **Collect fonts** can link each font file into a folder instead of copying it, so a show's font folder doesn't hold the same fonts twice. See [Linking fonts](/en/docs/typesetting/fonts#linking-fonts).
- **What Aegisub keeps in the file:** saving an ASS file keeps the video, audio, timecodes and keyframes files, the zoom, the frame and the active line, as Aegisub does, so the file opens with the same media in Aegisub. See [Saving](/en/docs/basics/files-and-formats#saving).
- **More in Script properties:** **Reading limits**, and **More** with **Synch point** and **Update details**. See [The file's properties](/en/docs/basics/files-and-formats#the-files-properties).
- **Styles for new files:** **File › Settings › Script properties › Files › Styles for new files** chooses the style catalogue **File › New** starts with, and a Project can choose its own. See [Catalogues](/en/docs/basics/styles#catalogues).
- **Autosave every** sets how soon after a change the autosave runs, from 5 seconds to 1 hour. **File › Open video…** opens `.y4m` files.

#### Shortcuts and Discord

- **Aegisub shortcuts:** **Import settings…** brings over the keyboard shortcuts of Aegisub 3.1 and later, and lists any Aegisub command HikariSub doesn't have yet. See [Shortcuts from Aegisub](/en/docs/get-started/moving-from-legacy#shortcuts-from-aegisub).
- **Discord presence your way:** choose what shows, write your own two lines with placeholders, go idle or clear after a while, and choose what a Project shows, in **File › Settings › Updates and privacy › Discord**. See [Discord presence](/en/docs/help/what-is-sent#discord-presence).

#### Plugins

- **Plugins can work longer and in the background:** a plugin command that runs past 10 seconds is no longer stopped. A card says the plugin is still working, with **Stop**. A plugin whose install question says *Works in the background while HikariSub is open* starts with HikariSub and can work on a timer, and the status bar counts the plugins at work, each with **Stop**. A plugin may use up to 2 GB of memory. See [Safety and limits](/en/docs/plugins/using-plugins#safety-and-limits).

#### Automation

- **Macros that can't run are greyed out** when the Automation menu opens, with the reason under the row, and macros that report themselves active get a check mark, as in Aegisub. See [Macros that can't run here](/en/docs/automation/running-scripts#macros-that-cant-run-here).
- **Undo points:** macros that call `aegisub.set_undo_point` get one undo step for each point, as in Aegisub. See [Undo points](/en/docs/automation/writing-scripts#undo-points).
- **MoonScript 0.7.0 and Aegisub-Motion 1.1.2** come with HikariSub.

#### Faster start

- **HikariSub starts faster on Windows:** menu rows are made the first time their menu opens.

### Fixed

- **HikariSub no longer freezes while it saves the panel layout or autosaves** on a busy disk.
- **Dragging a side bar's inner edge keeps the new width** when you let go; it used to snap back.

## 0.1.0-beta.4

Published 2026-11-17. [Release notes](https://github.com/altqx/hikari/releases/tag/v0.1.0-beta.4)

### Introducing

#### Projects and the Explorer

- **Projects:** open a folder as a Project with **File › Open folder…**, the Home page, a dropped folder, `hikarisub <folder>`, or **Open with HikariSub** on a folder in Windows Explorer. The Project keeps what a show shares in its `.hikari` folder: settings, which **File › Settings** shows on its own **Project** tab, fonts, dictionary words, style and font catalogues, layouts, presets, scripts, plugin settings and AI keys. Everyone who opens the folder gets the same fonts, terms and tools, while your own settings stay yours. See [Projects](/en/docs/basics/files-and-formats#projects) and [Project tiers](/en/docs/basics/project-tiers).
- **The Explorer:** the **Explorer** panel, in **View › Explorer**, shows the Project's folder, where you can open, rename, trash or add subtitle files. It opens with a Project, so the files of an episode or a whole show are one click away. See [the Explorer](/en/docs/basics/projects#the-explorer).
- **Trusted Projects:** a Project folder that brings its own scripts or plugin settings asks you to trust it first, with **Trust folder** or **Not now**. Until then, the Automation panel and the plugin manager show a lock. See [Project scripts](/en/docs/basics/files-and-formats#project-scripts).

#### Side bars

- **Side bars:** VS Code-style side bars keep your tools at the window's edges: the AI side bar on the left holds the Agent and Review panels, and the tools side bar on the right holds the Explorer, Find, Shift times, the Timing postprocessor, Styles and QC notes. Move any tool panel into a side bar from its **⋯** menu, toggle them from the title bar, the **View** menu or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd>, and switch their edges with **View › Swap side bars**. On the first start, panels docked right of the Grid move into the side bars once. Windows narrower than 2140 px start with the tools side bar alone, and with the default side bars, panels still fit on 1280×800 and 1366×768 screens. See [Side bars](/en/docs/customize/layouts#side-bars).

#### Several windows

- **More than one window:** drag a tab out of the tab row into a new window, or onto another window's tab row, or use **Move tab to new window** and **Move tab to window ›**. Each window has its own tabs, panels, layout, video and audio, and notices about a tab show in that tab's window. <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>F4</kbd> now runs **Close window**, which asks only about that window's changes, and sessions bring back every window. See [Windows](/en/docs/customize/layouts#windows) and [Closing windows](/en/docs/customize/layouts#closing-windows).

#### Hikari AI and agents

- **The Agent panel and the Review panel:** ask HikariSub's AI agent to work on your subtitles in threads, on a provider you add or on Claude Code, Codex or an ACP agent such as OpenCode or Antigravity already on your computer. Every edit waits in the **Review panel**, and in the Grid and the Line editor, until you **Keep** or **Undo** it. Each thread has a mode, Manual, Auto or Full access, risky actions ask in every mode, and each thread and each day have a cost cap. The model button names the model the next message uses, and its menu also offers the models of Claude Code, Codex and ACP agents. See [The Agent panel](/en/docs/ai/agent) and [Reviewing AI changes](/en/docs/ai/review).
- **What the agent can do:** it can time karaoke, typeset signs with HikariSub's own tools, build node effects, set keyframes, write and run Lua scripts and plugins, search the web, use MCP servers' tools, and split work across sub-agents. Running a script or installing a plugin always asks first, in every mode. In a Project, it reads the Project's files and `AGENTS.md`, and its file changes wait for your **Keep** too. See [The Agent panel](/en/docs/ai/agent).
- **Skills:** skills are instructions for kinds of work that the agent reads when your request matches one. Three come with HikariSub, typesetting, translation and karaoke-templater, and you and a Project can add your own. See [Skills](/en/docs/ai/skills).
- **AI inline actions and Translate:** **AI actions ›** in the Grid's and the Line editor's right-click menus runs one task on the lines you choose, with no Agent panel thread: **Rewrite…**, **Shorten to 15 CPS**, **Fix spelling and grammar**, **Translate to** and your own actions. Translate runs a translator and a reviewer, keeps names and terms from Honya's `GLOSSARY.md` and `CHARACTERS.md`, and in Translation mode marks the Lines the reviewer didn't approve Unconfirmed. See [Inline actions](/en/docs/ai/inline-actions) and [Translating](/en/docs/ai/inline-actions#translating).
- **Connect your own agent:** **File › Settings › AI › MCP › Let agents outside HikariSub edit through MCP** lets Claude Code, Codex or another agent you run yourself read and edit your open files, and its edits wait in the Review panel too. **Copy setup** copies what the agent needs. See [Connect Claude Code or Codex](/en/docs/ai/connect-an-agent).
- **AI settings and privacy:** **File › Settings › AI** holds your providers, external agents, MCP servers, skills and inline actions, and keeps the AI component up to date; if the component is missing, **Get the AI component** in the Agent panel downloads it, with its progress. AI never sees your recent files or your privacy settings, and the Agent panel is translated into Korean, Polish, Tamil and Thai. See [AI settings](/en/docs/ai/settings) and [What is sent](/en/docs/help/what-is-sent#ai).

#### Node effects

- **Node effects:** build karaoke effects as a graph of nodes on a canvas, in a mode of its own: **View › Node effects**, or the button next to the layout switcher. The current line previews on the video after every edit, nothing is written until **Apply**, and all ten karaoke presets come as graphs. Nodes cover text to drawings, combining drawings, highlights, inline-fx, audio peaks, strips, copies and particles, and a graph can be exported as karaOK or Aegisub 3.2.2 template lines. The Node effects layout puts the Node panel under the Graph. See [Node effects](/en/docs/karaoke/node-effects).
- **Typesetting graphs:** node effects run on ordinary lines too. Select lines, choose **Subtitles › Typesetting graph…**, and pick one of ten ready-made graphs, such as Shake, Typewriter or Chromatic aberration; the original line is kept as a comment. See [Typesetting graphs](/en/docs/karaoke/node-effects#typesetting-graphs).

#### Automation and native tools

- **The karaoke templater, built in, with karaOK:** HikariSub runs Karaoke Templater natively, making the same lines as before, and also runs templates written for karaOK. It comes with ten karaOK presets and `ln.kara`, karaOK's template library, by logarithm with petzku, and the Template editor shows and switches a file's **Template language**. Thai support is by Jibrill. See [karaOK templates](/en/docs/karaoke/templater#karaok-templates) and [The ln.kara library](/en/docs/karaoke/templater#the-lnkara-library).
- **Motion and perspective tracking:** **Video › Tracking** does what Aegisub-Motion does: **Apply motion data…** moves the selected lines with After Effects keyframe data from Mocha or Blender, and **Revert motion data** puts them back. **Apply Power-Pin data…** applies Power-Pin data frame by frame, and **Video › Track colour…** and **Find fade…** follow a colour or a fade on the video. See [Motion tracking](/en/docs/typesetting/motion-tracking) and [Perspective tracking](/en/docs/typesetting/perspective#perspective-tracking).
- **Native typesetting tools:** what used to need scripts is now built in, with a preview and one undo step each. The **Gradient** and **Text on path** visual tools, **Pathfinder** and **Offset** for drawings, **Subtitles › Blur and glow layers…**, **Remove tags…** and **Interpolate across lines…** do natively what the popular scripts do. See [Gradients](/en/docs/typesetting/visual-tools#gradients), [Blur and glow layers](/en/docs/typesetting/visual-tools#blur-and-glow-layers) and [Removing tags](/en/docs/basics/grid#removing-tags).
- **QC notes:** **Subtitles › QC notes** opens a panel for the notes of a QC pass, kept in the subtitle file itself, where Aegisub keeps them too. Each note has a time, a section and a **Done** box, and the Grid flags the line it marks. It imports arch1t3cht's Note Browser and mpvQC notes, and exports Note Browser's. See [QC notes](/en/docs/checking/qc-notes).
- **Export to other formats:** **File › Export as…** writes a copy as SubRip (SRT), WebVTT, YouTube SBV, plain text, MicroDVD, MPL2, TMPlayer, TTML (IMSC1), DFXP or YouTube timed text (srv3), and the file you are editing stays as it is. A report lists each line that lost something the format can't carry. See [Export](/en/docs/basics/export).

#### Plugins

- **Plugins:** **Automation › Plugins** installs plugins, shows what each asks for and who signed it, and turns them on or off. A plugin can add commands, panels, dialogs and node-effects nodes, and ordinary plugins run in an operating-system sandbox, asking before they use a folder. Full trust plugins run outside it. See [Using plugins](/en/docs/plugins/using-plugins).
- **The plugin marketplace:** the plugin manager's **Marketplace** view lists plugins from the HikariSub marketplace, with search and categories, and **Install in HikariSub** on the marketplace website opens HikariSub at a plugin. Marketplace plugins update themselves, and an update that asks for more access waits for **Review…**. See [From the marketplace](/en/docs/plugins/using-plugins#from-the-marketplace).
- **HikariSub's own plugins:** [Macro chains](/en/docs/plugins/using-plugins#macro-chains) records commands and Automation macros as a chain and runs it again as one undo step. [Keyframe generator](/en/docs/plugins/using-plugins#keyframe-generator) finds the scene changes in the open video as a background job you can cancel, then sets them as the video's keyframes or saves an Aegisub keyframes file. [Git line marks](/en/docs/plugins/using-plugins#git-line-marks) marks the lines added, changed or removed since a git branch, tag or commit. The Plugins window and the marketplace website mark them **By HikariSub**.
- **For plugin authors:** [Write your first plugin](/en/docs/plugins/write-your-first-plugin) builds a plugin in three steps, and the [Hikari API reference](/en/docs/plugins/api-reference) lists every operation, event and type. Plugins can run long jobs, group steps into one undo step, mark Grid rows, run macros, read video frames and keyframes, read the Project's folder and, with `vcs.read`, read git. See [Long jobs](/en/docs/plugins/write-your-first-plugin#long-jobs).

#### Also new

- **Discord presence:** HikariSub can show what you are doing in your Discord status, so friends can see you're subbing. It is off by default, and the file name shows only if you turn that on too. See [Discord presence](/en/docs/help/what-is-sent#discord-presence).
- **A suggestion to star HikariSub on GitHub:** after real use, a quiet message suggests starring HikariSub on GitHub, which helps others find it. It shows at most twice ever, and never again once you press **Star on GitHub**. See [Star on GitHub suggestion](/en/docs/help/what-is-sent#star-on-github-suggestion).

### New and improved

#### Menus and shortcuts

- **Grid and Line editor menus:** the Grid's right-click menu is in seven groups and goes at most two levels deep. A new **Typesetting ›** holds **Blur and glow layers…**, **Build layers with the last values**, **Typesetting graph…**, **Interpolate across lines…**, **Position to margins** and **Margins to position**. **AI actions ›** has a group of its own, and **Add QC note…** sits with the view rows, so **Delete text** and **Delete lines** end the menu. **Convert Aegisub folds to line groups** shows only when the file has Aegisub folds, and is also in **Subtitles**. A greyed-out row says why it's unavailable: **Interpolate across lines…**, for example, needs three selected lines. In the Line editor, the switch is **Check spelling as you type**, and the add-word rows name the dictionary: **Add “…” to project dictionary** or **to my dictionary**. **Align and distribute ›** has **Align** and **Distribute** headings with shorter rows, such as **Left edges** and **Horizontally**. See [The right-click menu](/en/docs/basics/grid#the-right-click-menu).
- **Video, audio and node effects menus:** in the video's right-click menu, **Full screen on ›** lists every monitor, the first one included, and in full screen it marks the one you're on. **Recently opened ›** has no empty headings and is hidden when nothing is recent, **Change aspect ratio…** gets its "…", and chapter times have no brackets. The audio play commands have one name everywhere, in the menus, the button tooltips and **File › Settings › Shortcuts**, such as **Play before the mark**, **Play the first 500 ms** and **Play 500 ms after the end**. **Customise the audio panel** shows no keys for its show and hide rows. In node effects, the graph's menu button is **⋯** (**More options**), **From the library** has no "…", **From a preset ›** groups its graphs under *For karaoke lines* and *For selected lines*, and the **Export as template lines** rows simply export. See [The right-click menu](/en/docs/basics/video#the-right-click-menu) and [The mode bar](/en/docs/karaoke/node-effects#the-mode-bar).
- **Explorer, QC notes and tab bar menus:** the Explorer's right-click menu is in groups, with **Move to the trash…** last, and its **⋯** menu holds **New subtitle file**, **Refresh**, **Group by name** and **Show all files**, so they stay within reach in a narrow panel. The QC notes **⋯** menu reads **Import notes from the clipboard…** and **Delete all notes…**, which sits on its own and asks with a **Delete all** button, and the empty panel offers **Import from the clipboard**. Right-clicking the tab bar's empty part no longer offers **Move tab to new window** or **Move tab to window ›**. **Manage layouts** loses its "…". See [The right-click menu](/en/docs/basics/projects#the-right-click-menu) and [Removing notes](/en/docs/checking/qc-notes#removing-notes).
- **Presets and ⋯ menus:** in an Automation script's **⋯ › Presets**, as in Shift times and the Timing postprocessor, **Delete preset** is now the last row, after a separator.
