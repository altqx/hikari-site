HikariSub comes with twenty karaoke effects, ready to use. A preset is a set of template and code lines. The [Template editor](/en/docs/karaoke/template-editor) inserts them into your file, and from then on they are ordinary templates: you preview, edit and apply them like your own.

The first ten are stock Karaoke Templater syntax, with no extra libraries, so a file that uses them gives the same lines in Aegisub 3.2.2. The other ten are written in [karaOK](/en/docs/karaoke/templater#karaok-templates)'s template language, and three of them load [ln.kara](/en/docs/karaoke/templater#the-lnkara-library); in Aegisub, they need the karaOK templater. All of them were written for HikariSub.

## Inserting a preset

In the Template editor, press **New**, then **From a preset**. The presets are listed by name; hover one to read what it does, in a tooltip and in the status bar. Click it to insert it.

The karaOK presets are marked with an (i). In a file whose templates follow Aegisub 3.2.2, they are turned off, with the reason: *Uses karaOK template syntax. Switch Template language to karaOK to use it.* See [The template language](/en/docs/karaoke/template-editor#the-template-language). In a file with no templates and no `Karaoke Templater` line yet, a karaOK preset goes in with `Karaoke Templater: karaOK`, in the same undo step.

In a karaOK file, the first ten are put in karaOK's words as they go in: `pre-line` becomes `line`, `line` becomes `lsyl`, and `syl` with `char` becomes `char`, so Falling letters' `template syl noblank char` goes in as `template char noblank`. Code lines stay as they are.

The first ten are also in [Node effects](/en/docs/karaoke/node-effects#presets) as graphs, under **From a preset** on the mode bar.

The preset's lines go where **New** puts a template:

- above the first karaoke line, in the preset's order, each with its own layer;
- with the style of that karaoke line;
- as comments, so nothing is generated yet.

The first of them becomes the current template, and the preview shows the effect at once. If the panel is still asking whether to trust the file's templates, the preview waits until you press **Preview**, as for any template.

A preset is added to the templates already in the file; it replaces nothing. The insertion is one undo step, *New template from preset*, so **Edit › Undo** takes all its lines out again. Then press **Apply** to generate the effect for the whole song.

## The presets

| Preset | Lines it adds | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Classic fill** | `template pre-line fill`, `template line fill` | The line fills from left to right as it is sung (`\kf`), in the style's own colours. It fades in 0.3 s before the line and out 0.3 s after it. |
| **Staggered fade** | `template syl noblank` | The syllables fade in one after another before the line starts, turn amber as they are sung, and fade out together after it ends. |
| **Colour sweep** | Two `template syl noblank`, on layers 0 and 1 | An orange copy of each syllable wipes across it from left to right while it is sung, with an animated `\clip`. |
| **Scale pop** | `template syl noblank` | Each syllable swells to 130 % of its style's scale as it is sung, turns pale yellow, and settles back to the style's size. |
| **Bounce** | Four `template syl noblank` | Each syllable hops up 16 pixels and lands again while it is sung, and stays pale yellow until the line ends. |
| **Glow** | Two `template syl noblank`, on layers 0 and 1 | A soft blue halo flares behind each syllable as it is sung, then fades; the syllable turns pale blue. |
| **Sparkles** | `template syl noblank`, and `template syl noblank notext loop 8` on layer 1 | Eight small sparks fly out from each syllable as it is sung. The sparks are a drawing, not text. |
| **Echo** | Two `template syl noblank`, on layers 0 and 1 | A copy of each syllable grows to 180 % of its style's scale behind it and fades away as it is sung. |
| **Falling letters** | `template syl noblank char` | The letters drop into place one by one, from left to right, before the line starts, then turn light green syllable by syllable. |
| **Colour per line** | `code once`, `template pre-line palette`, `template line palette` | Like Classic fill, but the lines take turns through a palette of four fill colours, orange, blue, green and pink, by their place in the file. |

The colours are written in the template text as `\1c` and `\3c` tags, and Colour per line keeps its palette in the `code once` line. Change them there.

### karaOK presets

| Preset | Lines it adds | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Word pop** | `template word noblank` | Each word swells as it starts and settles back while it is sung. |
| **Word wipe** | `template line wipe`, `template lword wipe`, and `template word noblank` on layer 1 | The line shows whole, and a second colour wipes across each word while it is sung. |
| **Letter cascade** | `template line cascade`, `template lchar cascade` | Letters appear one after another along the line before it is sung, timed by `$ci`. |
| **Letter shimmer** | `template char noblank` | Each letter tilts and brightens as it is sung, then straightens. |
| **Furigana rise** | `template syl noblank`, `template furichar` | Furigana rises into place over each syllable as it is sung. It needs [readings](/en/docs/karaoke/furigana) such as `漢\|かん`. |
| **Early lead-in** | `template syl noblank` | Each syllable lights up a moment before it is sung, with `k_retime`. |
| **Wave bounce** | Two `code once`, `template syl noblank` | Each syllable bobs up and down on a sine wave while it is sung, with `ln.wave`. |
| **Shape burst** | `code once`, `template syl noblank`, and `template syl noblank notext loop 6` on layer 1 | Six stars burst out of each syllable as it is sung. The stars are `ln.shapes.star` drawings. |
| **Line gradient** | `code once`, `template line gradient`, `template lchar gradient` | The line's letters run through a band of colours from left to right, with `ln.color.byHSL` and `$llen`. |
| **Thai fade** | `template char noblank` | Each character cluster fades in as it is sung, so Thai vowels and tone marks stay on their letters. Thai support is by Jibrill. |

Wave bounce, Shape burst and Line gradient load ln.kara in their `code once` lines.

The presets use no random values, and nothing in them carries over from one karaoke line to the next. So the preview of any line shows what **Apply** writes for it, and **Apply to the selected lines** can regenerate one line on its own.

## Making a preset your own

Once inserted, a preset's lines are yours: change the tags, the timings or the Effect in the panel, and the preview follows.

The list itself is built into HikariSub. You can't add your own presets to it, or change or remove the ones in it. To reuse templates in another file, copy their lines in the [Grid](/en/docs/basics/grid#copying-and-pasting) and paste them there.

## What to check

- **Other styles get nothing.** A template applies only to karaoke lines of its own style. If the song has karaoke lines in several styles, change each preset line's **Style**, or add `all` to its Effect.
- **Two presets draw twice.** Inserting a second preset keeps the first, and both run. Delete the lines of the one you don't want, or undo it.
- **Sizes are in script pixels.** The hops, drops and sparks move a fixed number of pixels, chosen for a 1280 × 720 script. At a larger script resolution they look smaller; change the numbers in the template text.
- **Times go past the line.** Most presets start their lines up to 0.8 s before the karaoke line and end them after it, to fade in and out. Leave room between lines, or shorten the `retime` values.

The presets add no fonts or styles: they draw in the karaoke line's style. Like any template, they need a script resolution, and the templater asks for one if the file has none: see [Running it](/en/docs/karaoke/templater#running-it).
