The **Getting started** window walks through the settings most people change first: the language, the theme, the layout, video and audio, editing, updates and usage data. Each step is a page, and each page says at its foot where its choice lives later, so nothing here is set for good.

## When it opens

It opens by itself the first time HikariSub starts, when there are no settings yet. Once you answer it, with **Finish** or **Skip setup**, it doesn't open by itself again. If HikariSub is closed before you answer, it opens again at the next start.

To open it later, choose **Help › Getting started**. It has no key at first; you can give it one in [Hotkeys](/en/docs/customize/hotkeys). Opened again, the pages show your settings as they are now.

<kbd>F1</kbd>, or the **?** in its title bar, opens this page.

The dots at the top show the step you are on. There are seven steps, or eight when HikariSub finds HikariSub 0.0.x (*legacy*) on the computer.

- **Next** goes to the next step. On the last step it becomes **Finish**.
- **Back** goes to the step before.
- **Skip setup** closes the window.

Nothing is saved until **Finish**. The language and the theme change while you choose them, so you can see them, but they go back if you don't finish.

**Finish** saves every step's choices and, if you picked a layout, shows your tab in it.

**Skip setup**, <kbd>Esc</kbd> and closing the window all do the same: nothing you chose is saved, and nothing is imported. When the window opened by itself at the first start, a notice says that the default settings are in use and that **Help › Getting started** opens the setup again.

## The steps

### Language

The first step, **Welcome to HikariSub**, lists the interface languages. The window switches to the language you pick, so you can read the rest of it in that language.

Later: **File › Settings › General › Language › Interface language**.

### Import from legacy

This step shows only when HikariSub finds legacy on this computer. It reads its settings and changes nothing in them.

- **Import my old settings**, chosen at first, shows the legacy settings that differ from HikariSub's, with their legacy value and their value now. The legacy values HikariSub proposes are ticked; tick or untick any. **All** shows every setting read, and the search field finds one.
- **Set up from scratch** starts from HikariSub's defaults.

If some legacy files are not UTF-8, choose how to read them under **Files that are not UTF-8 are read as**: Latin-1 or Windows-1252.

When you import, the next steps start from the imported values. A change you make on a later step wins over the imported value, even if you go back to this step.

At **Finish**, the steps' own settings are saved at once. The rest of the import is staged and takes effect the next time HikariSub starts, as **File › Import legacy settings…** does. What can come over, and what can't, is in [Moving from legacy](/en/docs/get-started/moving-from-legacy#what-comes-over). Legacy themes are not imported.

If the import can't be staged, for example because the legacy files changed since they were read, **Finish** saves nothing and takes you back to this step with the reason. **Read again** reads the files again.

The window takes the first legacy installation it finds. To import from another, such as a portable copy, use **File › Import legacy settings…** later, which can also roll an import back.

Later: **File › Import legacy settings…**

### Look

- **Follow system theme** uses Light or Dark as your system does.
- The two samples, **Light** and **Dark**, pick a theme. Picking one turns off Follow system theme.
- **Accent colour** picks one of seven accents for the theme shown. Light and Dark keep their own.
- **Enable high contrast** switches to High contrast white, or High contrast black from Dark. Its colours are set in Settings.
- **Title bar** chooses between **HikariSub's** title bar and **The system's**, with a note on which suits your desktop. It shows only where HikariSub can draw its own title bar.

The whole window changes as you choose.

Later: **File › Settings › Appearance**. See [Themes](/en/docs/customize/themes).

### What do you mostly do?

This step picks the [layout](/en/docs/customize/layouts) your first tab opens with. Each card shows the layout's arrangement:

| Card | Layout |
| --- | --- |
| **Time** | Timing |
| **Translate** | Translation |
| **Typeset** | Typesetting |
| **Karaoke** | Karaoke, which also turns on the Audio panel's Karaoke mode |

Click a card again to unpick it. With none picked, the tab keeps Editing, which has a bit of everything.

Later: **View › Layout**, or the layout switcher in the title bar.

- **Subtitles drawn by** chooses the subtitle renderer. Where libass is the only one, as on Linux, it says so. The sample frame below is drawn by the renderer chosen.
- **Sound plays on** chooses the output device. **Test sound** plays a short tone on it.

Later: **File › Settings › Video › Subtitles on video › Subtitle display filter**, and **File › Settings › Audio › Output › Device**.

### Editing

- **Autosave files kept**: how many autosaved copies of each file HikariSub keeps, from 2, 3 at first. See [Autosave and recovery](/en/docs/basics/files-and-formats#autosave-and-recovery).
- **Check spelling** and the **Primary language** beside it. The **Example** line marks its misspelt word when spelling is on and the dictionary is installed.
- **Grammar**: **Off**, chosen at first, or the English to check grammar in, with Harper. See [Grammar](/en/docs/checking/grammar). The **Example** line shows spelling marks only.

Later: **File › Settings › General › Saving**, and **File › Settings › Spelling & grammar**. See [Spelling](/en/docs/checking/spelling).

- **Stable versions only**, chosen at first, offers tested releases.
- **Pre-releases too** offers beta versions as well.
- **Check for updates automatically** looks for a new version at most once a day, when HikariSub starts. It is off at first. The release notes on the Home page need it too.
- **Send anonymous usage data** sends which features are used, errors and crashes, and how fast things open. It is on at first. Untick it to turn it off; **What is sent** opens the full list, [What is sent](/en/docs/help/what-is-sent), in your browser.

Skipping setup leaves it as it is, which at the first start means on.

Later: **File › Settings › Updates and privacy**. See [Updates](/en/docs/get-started/install#updates).

### Your files

The last step shows your [user folder](/en/docs/automation/user-folder), which holds your settings, hotkeys, scripts, dictionaries and saved layouts. **Open folder** opens it in your file manager. Copy it to take your setup to another computer.

Later: **File › Settings › General › Folders**, or **Help › Open user folder**.
