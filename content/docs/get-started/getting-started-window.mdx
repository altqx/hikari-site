The **Getting started** window walks through the settings most people change first: the language, how HikariSub looks, the layout, video and audio, editing, AI, and updates and privacy. Each step ends with a line that says where its settings live later, so nothing here is set for good.

## When it opens

It opens by itself the first time HikariSub starts, when there are no settings yet, before the [Home page](/en/docs/get-started/tour#the-home-page). Once you answer it, with **Finish** or **Skip setup**, it doesn't open by itself again. If HikariSub is closed before you answer, it opens again at the next start.

To open it later, choose **Help › Getting started**. It has no key at first; you can give it one in [Shortcuts](/en/docs/customize/hotkeys). Opened again, the steps show your settings as they are now.

<kbd>F1</kbd>, or the **?** in its title bar, opens this page.

The steps are listed down the left side of the window: nine, or eight when HikariSub finds no old settings to bring over. The current step is highlighted, and a tick marks each step you have seen. Click a step to go to it, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>PgDn</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>PgUp</kbd> for the next or the previous one.

- **Next**, or <kbd>Enter</kbd>, goes to the next step. On the last step it becomes **Finish**.
- **Back**, or <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>←</kbd>, goes to the step before.
- **Skip setup**, or <kbd>Esc</kbd>, closes the window and keeps HikariSub's defaults.

On steps with cards, the arrow keys move between the cards and <kbd>Space</kbd> chooses one.

Nothing is saved until **Finish**. The language and the look change while you choose them, so you can see them, but they go back if you don't finish. **Finish** saves every step's choices and opens your first tab in the layout you picked.

Each step's **Later** line names where its settings are in **File › Settings**. Click it to close the window, keeping your choices as **Finish** would, and open Settings at that place.

**Skip setup**, <kbd>Esc</kbd> and closing the window all do the same: nothing you chose is saved, and nothing is imported. When the window opened by itself at the first start, a notice says that the default settings are in use and that **Help › Getting started** opens the setup again.

## The steps

### Welcome

The first step, **Welcome to HikariSub**, lists the interface languages. The window switches to the language you pick, so you can read the rest of it in that language.

Later: **File › Settings › General › Language › Interface language**.

### Bring over [#import-from-legacy]

This step shows only when HikariSub finds old settings on this computer. It reads them and changes nothing in them.

- **Import my old settings**, chosen at first, shows the old settings that differ from HikariSub's, with their old value and their value now. The values HikariSub proposes are ticked; tick or untick any. **All** shows every setting read, and the search field finds one.
- **Set up from scratch** starts from HikariSub's defaults.

If some files are not UTF-8, choose how to read them under **Files that are not UTF-8 are read as**: Latin-1 or Windows-1252.

When you import, the next steps start from the imported values. A change you make on a later step wins over the imported value, even if you go back to this step. Leaving this step by any route, **Next** or a click in the step list, applies the import to the steps that follow.

At **Finish**, the steps' own settings are saved at once. The rest of the import is staged and takes effect the next time HikariSub starts, as **File › Settings › General › Import settings…** does. What can come over, and what can't, is in [Moving from legacy](/en/docs/get-started/moving-from-legacy#what-comes-over). Legacy themes are not imported.

If the import can't be staged, for example because the files changed since they were read, **Finish** saves nothing and takes you back to this step with the reason. **Read again** reads the files again.

The window takes the first legacy installation it finds. To import from another, such as a portable copy or Aegisub's shortcuts, use **File › Settings › General › Import settings…** later. **Roll back the last import…**, beside it, rolls an import back.

Later: **File › Settings › General › Import settings…**

### Look

The whole window changes as you choose.

- **Follow system theme** uses Light or Dark as your system does, and **High contrast** switches to a high-contrast theme.
- **Built in** shows HikariSub's themes as cards, each with a sample of its colours. Click one to choose it.
- **From plugins** shows the colour themes of the [plugins](/en/docs/plugins/using-plugins) you have installed, each with its plugin's name. The last card, **More themes…**, opens the plugin marketplace at its themes once the window closes, and keeps your choices so far.
- **Accent colour** picks one of the theme's accents.
- **Icons** chooses **HikariSub (built in)** or the icon theme of a plugin you have installed. The window shows the icons as you choose.
- **Title bar** chooses between **HikariSub's** title bar and the system's, with a note on which suits your desktop. It shows only where HikariSub can draw its own title bar.

Later: **File › Settings › Appearance**. See [Themes](/en/docs/customize/themes).

### How you work

**How do you mostly work?** picks the [layout](/en/docs/customize/layouts) your first tab opens with. Each card shows the layout's arrangement:

| Card | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Editing**, chosen at first | Video, audio, the Line editor and the Grid: a bit of everything |
| **Translate** | The Translation layout, with the original above your translation and Translation mode on |
| **Time** | The Timing layout, with the audio across the top |
| **Typeset** | The Typesetting layout: a large video with the visual tools and the Styles panel |
| **Karaoke** | The Karaoke layout, with the Template editor beside the video and the Audio panel in Karaoke mode |
| **Full screen** | The Typesetting layout, with your first tab in [full-screen typesetting](/en/docs/basics/video#full-screen-typesetting) |

Later: **View › Layout**, or the layout switcher in the title bar.

- **Subtitles drawn by** chooses the subtitle renderer. Where libass is the only one, as on Linux, it says so. The sample frame below is drawn by the renderer chosen.
- **Sound plays on** chooses the output device. **Test sound** plays a short tone on it.

Later: **File › Settings › Video › Subtitles on video › Subtitle display filter**, and **File › Settings › Audio › Output › Device**.

### Editing

- **Autosave files kept**: how many autosaved copies of each file HikariSub keeps, from 2, 3 at first. See [Autosave and recovery](/en/docs/basics/files-and-formats#autosave-and-recovery).
- **Check spelling** and the **Primary language** beside it. The **Example** line marks its misspelt word when spelling is on and the dictionary is installed.
- **Grammar**: **Off**, chosen at first, or the English to check grammar in, with Harper. See [Grammar](/en/docs/checking/grammar). The **Example** line shows spelling marks only.

Later: **File › Settings › General › Saving**, and **File › Settings › Spelling and grammar**. See [Spelling](/en/docs/checking/spelling).

### AI

HikariSub can help with translation, review and an agent that edits with you. AI stays off unless you turn it on here or later.

- **Keep AI off**, chosen at first: no AI menus, panels or downloads, and nothing about your subtitles leaves this computer.
- **Turn AI on**: after **Finish**, HikariSub downloads its AI component once. You then add a provider, or sign in to an agent, in **File › Settings › AI**.

Later: **File › Settings › AI**. See [Turning AI on](/en/docs/ai/settings#turning-ai-on) and [What is sent](/en/docs/help/what-is-sent#ai).

- **Stable versions only** offers tested releases only. On a stable release it is chosen at first and marked *Recommended*.
- **Pre-releases too** offers beta versions as well. On a beta or release candidate it is chosen at first.
- **Check for updates automatically** looks for a new version at most once a day, when HikariSub starts. It is off at first.
- **Send anonymous usage data** sends which features are used, errors and crashes, and how fast things open. It is on at first. Untick it to turn it off; **What is sent** opens the full list, [What is sent](/en/docs/help/what-is-sent), in your browser. Skipping setup leaves it as it is, which at the first start means on.
- **Show what I am doing in Discord**, off at first, shows the mode you're in as your Discord status: never your folders, your subtitles or the video. Its other choices are in Settings. See [Discord presence](/en/docs/help/what-is-sent#discord-presence).

Later: **File › Settings › Updates and privacy**. See [Updates](/en/docs/get-started/install#updates).

### Ready

The last step, **You're set**, sums up your choices: the language, the look (theme, accent and icons), the layout, AI, updates, and your files. Click a row to go back to its step.

Under **Good to know**, two reminders: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>P</kbd> opens anything or runs any command, as in [Quick Open](/en/docs/basics/command-palette), and **Automation › Plugins** brings formats, presets and themes from the marketplace.

If **Finish** can't save, the window goes back to the Bring over step when there is one, with the reason. Without it, it stays on this step and shows the problem above the summary.

#### Your files

The **Your files** row shows your [user folder](/en/docs/automation/user-folder), which holds your settings, shortcuts, scripts, dictionaries and saved layouts. **Open folder** opens it in your file manager. Copy it to take your setup to another computer.

Later: **File › Settings › General › Folders**, or **Help › Open user folder**.
