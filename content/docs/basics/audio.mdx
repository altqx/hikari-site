The Audio panel shows the sound as a waveform or a spectrum, with the active line marked on it. You can play any part of it and set a line's times with the mouse. In the Editing layout it sits above the Line editor.

## Opening audio

When you open a video that has sound, its audio opens in the panel too, without indexing again. A video without sound closes the panel's audio. A [dummy video](/en/docs/basics/video#dummy-video) brings silence as long as itself.

The **Audio** menu can also open audio on its own:

- **Open audio:** WAV, W64, FLAC, AC3, AAC, OGG, MP3, MP4, M4A, MKV or AVI. Choose **All files** to open another kind.
- **Recently opened audio:** <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+click an entry to show the file in its folder.
- **Open audio from video:** the open video's sound again, after you opened another file.
- **Close audio.**

If the file has several audio tracks, **Choose the audio track** asks which one. Decoded audio is kept in a cache on disk, so the same file opens faster the next time.

## What the display shows

- The active line is the bright range, between its start and end boundaries. The lines before and after it are shaded.
- **Modified** at the top left means the times you set are not yet applied to the line. **Negative time** means the start is after the end.
- The ruler shows the time, with a line every second. Keyframes from the video show as lines, and the paused video's frame as a dashed line.
- Under the pointer, a line shows the time there.

**Waveform | Spectrum** switches between the waveform and a spectrogram. With the spectrum on, **Speech** brings out the frequencies of voices.

**Sync with video**, on at first, keeps the two together: while the video plays, the audio cursor follows it, and while the audio plays, the paused video shows the frame at the audio's time.

## Playing

| Button | Key | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Previous line** | <kbd>Left</kbd> or <kbd>Z</kbd> | Goes to the previous line and plays it |
| **Play** | <kbd>Down</kbd> or <kbd>S</kbd> | Plays the selection |
| **Play line** | <kbd>Up</kbd> or <kbd>R</kbd> | Plays the whole line |
| **Stop** | <kbd>H</kbd> | Stops. Pressed again, plays on from where it stopped |
| **Next line** | <kbd>Right</kbd> or <kbd>X</kbd> | Goes to the next line and plays it |

Playing audio pauses the video first. To move between lines without playing them, turn on **Don't play after changing the line** in **File › Settings › Audio**.

**Play around** plays a short range around the line. Its face plays the range you last chose from its arrow; each range also has a key that plays it without changing the face:

| Range | Key |
| --- | --- |
| Play 500 ms before the start | <kbd>Q</kbd> |
| Play the first 500 ms | <kbd>E</kbd> |
| Play the last 500 ms | <kbd>D</kbd> |
| Play 500 ms after the end | <kbd>W</kbd> |
| Play to the end | <kbd>T</kbd> |
| Play before the mark | <kbd>Num 0</kbd> |
| Play after the mark | <kbd>Num .</kbd> |

Right-click the ruler to set the mark. Playing around it uses **Play around the marker** in Settings, 1 second at first.

The speaker button holds the volume, the output device, and the **Speed**: 25%, 33%, 50%, 75% or 100%, with the pitch kept. <kbd>[</kbd> and <kbd>]</kbd> change the speed. **Audio settings…** opens the Audio page of Settings.

## Timing with the mouse

| Mouse | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Left click | Sets the start there. Drag on to set the end too |
| Right click | Sets the end there |
| Drag a boundary | Moves the start or the end |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd> held on release | Also moves the neighbouring line: the next line's start to this end, or the previous line's end to this start |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> while dragging | Turns snapping on, or off if it is on |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+click or middle click | Moves the video there, without timing |
| Drag the ruler | Scrolls the view |
| Right-click the ruler | Sets the mark |

Times are rounded to the centisecond. **Snap to keyframes** and **Snap to other lines**, under **File › Settings › Audio**, make a boundary jump to a keyframe or another line's boundary near it. Both are off at first.

**In** (<kbd>C</kbd>) moves the start 200 ms earlier and **Out** (<kbd>V</kbd>) the end 300 ms later. Settings sets both amounts.

## Applying the times

**Commit** (<kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>G</kbd>) writes the times into the line, or into every selected line, as one undo step. It then moves to the next line. On the last line it adds a new, empty line five seconds long after it. Turn off **Go to the next line after applying changes** to stay on the line instead.

**Automatically apply changes**, on at first, applies every change you make with the mouse, In and Out at once, so you rarely need Commit. Turn it off to try times first: they wait in the Line editor, the display shows **Modified**, and Commit applies them. The small **A** on the Commit button shows that it is on.

A selection that ends before it starts is refused.

## Moving around

- The wheel scrolls. <kbd>Shift</kbd>+wheel zooms in and out around the pointer, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+wheel stretches the waveform up and down.
- <kbd>A</kbd> and <kbd>F</kbd> scroll left and right.
- The **Zoom** button shows the zoom, such as **1.0×**, and opens sliders for both stretches with **Reset zoom**.
- **Auto-scroll**, on at first, scrolls to each line you select.
- When the line is out of view, a **Back to line** chip shows at the edge it lies past. It, or <kbd>B</kbd>, scrolls back to it.

## Karaoke

**Line | Karaoke** switches to Karaoke mode, which splits the line into syllables you time one by one, and adds **Auto split** and **Tap to time** to the row. Choosing the [Karaoke layout](/en/docs/customize/layouts) turns it on too, with the Audio panel across the whole window. The karaoke guides cover it.

## Keys and the pointer

The Audio panel's keys work while the pointer is over it, without clicking it first. Turn this off with **Hotkeys follow the panel under the pointer** in Settings. <kbd>Space</kbd> plays the video from the Audio panel too.

Most of its keys also work with the pointer over the Grid, except <kbd>Enter</kbd> and the arrow keys, which the Grid keeps. So <kbd>G</kbd>, <kbd>Z</kbd> and <kbd>X</kbd> commit and move between lines from the Grid. The [Hotkeys reference](/en/docs/reference/hotkeys#audio) lists them all.

## Arranging the buttons

The gear, **Customise the audio panel**, chooses what the row shows: the Play around button, each play on its own, **Auto-commit**, **Go to the next line after commit**, **Go to selection** and the zoom and volume sliders. **Classic arrangement** shows them all, as older versions did, and **Default arrangement** goes back. When the panel is too narrow, the buttons that don't fit move into its **⋯** menu.

## Settings

**File › Settings › Audio** holds the output device, the audio delay, the lead-in and lead-out, snapping, the karaoke options and the cache. The [Settings reference](/en/docs/reference/settings#audio) lists them with their defaults.

Its **Display** section chooses what the display draws: the time beside the cursor, the seconds markers, the selection background, the video position, keyframes, how thick the line boundaries are, and which **Inactive lines** are shaded: **None**, **Before and after the active** or **All visible**. The display changes as soon as you apply them.
