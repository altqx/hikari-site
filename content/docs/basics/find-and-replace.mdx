---
title: Find and replace
description: Find text in your lines, replace it one match at a time or all at once, and search whole folders of subtitle files, all from the Find panel.
---

The **Find** panel finds and replaces text in your subtitles. It stays open while you work: the find row at the top, the replace row under it, the options behind a button, and the results.

## Opening the panel

- **Edit › Find…** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd>) opens it with the replace row as you last left it.
- **Edit › Replace…** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>H</kbd>) opens it with the replace row shown.

Either one puts the cursor in the find field, which starts with your last search, selected, so you can type over it. Pressing the same key again while you are in the panel closes it; <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>H</kbd> with the replace row hidden shows it instead. <kbd>Esc</kbd> goes back to the Line editor and leaves the panel open.

The **▸** button at the start of the find row shows or hides the replace row.

**Edit › Find next** (<kbd>F3</kbd>) and **Edit › Find previous** (<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>F3</kbd>) repeat the last search, even with the panel closed.

## Finding

Type the text and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or click **Find next**. The next match's line becomes the active line, and the match is selected in the Line editor, ready to edit. <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>F3</kbd> goes on to the one after it, and <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>F3</kbd> back to the one before.

Beside the field, the count says where you are, such as **3 of 12**, or **5 matches** before you have moved to one, or **No matches**. It counts exactly the matches Find next goes through, with the options below, and changes as you type, change an option or edit the lines.

The search wraps without asking: after the last match it goes on from the first, and Find previous goes from the first to the last. If nothing matches anywhere, a notice says *Could not find* and the text.

Comment lines are skipped unless **Include comments** is on, and lines hidden by the Grid's filter are always skipped.

## Options

**Aa** (**Match case**, <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>C</kbd>) tells capitals from small letters, and **.\*** (**Regular expression**, <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>R</kbd>) reads the text as a [pattern](#regular-expressions). Both are inside the find field.

The rest are behind **Options**. While they are hidden, small chips under the field name each option that isn't at its default, and the button has a dot.

| Option | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Search in** | **This document**, **Open documents** (every open tab) or **Folder** ([below](#searching-a-folder)) |
| **Field** | **Text**, **Style**, **Actor** or **Effect**. In Translation mode, Text covers both the original and the translation |
| **Lines** | **All**, **Selected** or **From selected**: from the first selected line to the end. Only for This document |
| **Styles** | **All styles**, or the styles you tick in **Choose styles**, with their line counts |
| **In text** | **All text**, **Outside tags** (not inside `{…}` blocks) or **Inside tags** |
| **Match at** | **Anywhere**, **Start** (the match must start the field) or **End** (the match must end it) |
| **Include comments** | Searches comment lines too |

HikariSub remembers every option.

## Replacing

Show the replace row with **▸**, or open the panel with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>H</kbd>, and type the new text in **Replace with**.

- **Replace** (<kbd>Enter</kbd> in Replace with) replaces the selected match and finds the next one. Each is one undo step. If the text has changed since it was found, it searches again rather than overwrite something else.
- **Replace all** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd>) replaces every match as one undo step, then says how many it replaced. With **Selected** lines and chosen styles, a line must be both selected and of a chosen style. With **Open documents**, it replaces in every open tab, one undo step per tab.

<kbd>Up</kbd> and <kbd>Down</kbd> in either field go through your recent searches or replacements.

## Listing every match

**Find all** (<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd>) lists the matches, on the right of the panel when it is wide enough, or below the rows when it is narrow. A summary leads them, such as **12 matches in 9 lines**, then each match as **Line N:** with the match marked, under its tab's name. Double-click a row to go to that line, and click a header to fold its rows.

With the replace row shown, each match is struck through and followed by its replacement. Every row starts ticked. Untick the ones to leave alone, or use **Check all** and **Uncheck all**, then click **Replace N checked**, which uses the text in Replace with. Lines you edited since the search are skipped and noted in the Log. **Close results** hides the list.

## Searching a folder

With **Search in** set to **Folder**, the panel searches subtitle files on disk, open or not. Lines and Styles give way to:

- **Folder**, with **Choose folder…** to pick it.
- **Files**, the file patterns separated by semicolons, such as `*.ass; *.srt`. Left empty, it searches ASS, SRT, SUB, TXT and MPL2 files.
- **Subfolders** and **Hidden folders**, to go deeper.

**Find all** lists the matches under each file's path, with a summary such as **5 matches in 3 files**. Double-click one to open the file at that line.

The replace row then holds only **Replace in N files…**, in red, which changes the files on disk. It asks first, because **Undo cannot bring them back**. Before it changes a file, it puts a copy in a `ReplaceBackup` folder beside your settings. Only files with a match are written, and they are saved as UTF-8.

## Regular expressions

With **Regular expression** on, the find text is a pattern. `.` is any character, `\d` a digit, `(…)` a group, `|` either side, and so on.

In **Replace with**, `\1` to `\9` insert a group, and `&` the whole match. Write `\&` for a plain `&`.

For example, to turn `Mr.` and `Mrs.` into `Mr` and `Mrs`, search for `(Mrs?)\.` and replace with `\1`.

A pattern with a mistake shows the error under the find field, in red. Until you fix it, nothing is searched: the find and replace buttons are off, and <kbd>Enter</kbd> does nothing.

## Keys

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd> | Find… |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>H</kbd> | Replace… |
| <kbd>F3</kbd>, or <kbd>Enter</kbd> in the find field | Find next |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>F3</kbd>, or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> in the find field | Find previous |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> | Find all |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> in Replace with | Replace, then find the next match |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> | Replace all |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>C</kbd>, <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>R</kbd> | Match case, Regular expression |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Back to the Line editor; the panel stays open |

**Edit › Select lines** selects lines by their text in a window of its own, without replacing anything.
