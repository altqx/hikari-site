## Previews

Live ASS previews render with [AkariSub](https://github.com/altqx/akarisub) (MIT), which compiles libass (ISC), FreeType, HarfBuzz, FriBidi (LGPL 2.1 or later), fontconfig, Expat and Brotli to WebAssembly. Their notices and licence texts are in [AkariSub's third-party notices](/licenses/akarisub.txt), and their source is in the AkariSub repository.

The frames behind the previews are from [*Sprite Fright*](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sprite_Fright_-_Blender_Open_Movie-full_movie.webm) (2021), © Blender Foundation, [studio.blender.org](https://studio.blender.org), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They are cropped to 16:9.
