## If you know Aegisub

- **Files:** your ASS files open and save as they are, and lines other programs added are kept.
- **Styles:** the same styles; Aegisub's storages are catalogues here.
- **Tags:** the same ASS tags, drawn with libass.
- **Timing keys:** play, commit and next line work in the Audio panel and the Grid.
- **Scripts:** your Lua and MoonScript macros and DependencyControl run.

[Coming from Aegisub](/en/docs/get-started/coming-from-aegisub) has the full table, including the <ParityNotYet /> features that aren't there yet.
